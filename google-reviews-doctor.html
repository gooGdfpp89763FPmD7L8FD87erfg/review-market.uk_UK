Everything about Google reviews for a doctor

Looking for a doctor is not what it used to be

The phone book and an aunt's advice have had their day: Google does the steering now. A patient after a new clinician types "GP near me" or "dermatologist" followed by their town. What comes up are listings with their Google rating and their reviews. With no feedback, or a poor average, a doctor simply does not exist against better-ranked colleagues.

The weight of reviews in a patient's decision

Nothing is built between a doctor and a patient without trust, and that trust starts forming well before the appointment. A reader who runs into feedback about a clinician who "really listens", "crystal-clear explanations", "a welcoming practice" lowers their guard. That is exactly where the swing happens between the patient who calls and the one who carries on searching elsewhere.

Different text depending on the specialty

The content moves with your discipline. With a GP, it will be about availability and follow-up over time. With a dermatologist, the accuracy of the diagnosis. With a paediatrician, patience with the very young. Every Google review for a doctor borrows the right vocabulary, without ever stepping outside professional bounds.
