Working out how many reviews it takes to fix a rating

Improving a Google rating is arithmetic, not intuition. What shows on your listing is nothing more than the weighted average of your reviews. Two examples say it all: with 20 reviews averaging 3.0, count on around forty five-star reviews to aim at 4.3; with 50 reviews at 3.5, it takes around fifty to reach 4.2. We set the dose that fits your case.

Getting past 4 stars: what volume should you plan for?

That is the decisive threshold, the one below which most customers do not even look at you. Depending on how many reviews are already live, plan for between 20 and 80 five-star reviews to get across. The outlay is absorbed quickly: above 4 stars, the flow of new customers changes scale.
