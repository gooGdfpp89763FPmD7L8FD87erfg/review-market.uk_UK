The part reviews play in ranking your GMB listing

Local ranking rests on a trio of factors: relevance, distance and prominence. It is on that last one that Google My Business reviews make all the difference. Three things catch the algorithm's attention in particular: a solid volume of recent reviews, a high average and replies from the owner. Every positive review firms up your place in the local pack a little more.
