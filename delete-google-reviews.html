Can a negative Google review really disappear?

It can, provided you stay inside a precise framework. Google opens the door to reporting as soon as a comment breaches its policy: fabricated account, defamatory statements, spam, conflict of interest or inappropriate content. We put every review through the sieve, then start whichever procedure best fits to secure its removal.

Which comments can realistically be removed?

Five categories come off a Google listing most often: fake reviews planted by a rival, ones from people who never set foot on your premises, ones that tip into defamation or insult, ones tainted by a conflict of interest, and outright spam. Every case is examined before we settle on the approach.

How long should you allow to get a Google review removed?

Reckon on 3 to 14 days for a Google review to be deleted, the range depending on how difficult the case is. Where the policy breach is glaring, removal sometimes comes far sooner. You are kept informed at every stage.

And if Google digs its heels in?
